Grippers

Each gripper has a pick sequence (starts empty above the part, ends above it holding the part) and a place sequence (starts holding the part above the spot, ends empty above it). Record them with the arm by hand (Guide) or by jogging (Control). Waypoints are stop points; guarded moves move in a direction until the contact force is reached (done live in Control mode).

Gripper mass centre of mass from flange

The robot needs the gripper's mass to estimate contact forces correctly (guarded moves, presses, force limit). It is sent when the gripper is selected; Desk's end-effector setting comes on top of it.

Teach position
